# Legacy feature map

Evidence reviewed includes both supplied ZIPs, the Python source `azam_easy_accounts_v2.py`, all thirteen README and update text files, the launch/build/test batch scripts, and both register photos. The archive contains SQL schema and upgrade code embedded in the Python source. It contains no live SQLite database. Historical notes describe successive versions and sometimes disagree with the final source. The table identifies those differences.

| Feature | Original implementation and known problems | V4 replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Login and logout | Tkinter login queries a local users table with a shared, hardcoded plaintext password. | Auth.js, individual users and hashed credentials, revocable sessions, configurable company roles and auditable changes. |
| Company selection, creation, renaming | Active-only company picker uses a mutable selection. Startup renames the original three companies in place and creates missing starter companies. New companies get starter accounts. No user-company assignments or archive UI. | Permanent company IDs, matching starter companies and chart, scoped URLs, activation, company settings and explicit membership. |
| Chart of accounts | Lists and adds company accounts with unique code and name and five account types. Default controls for cash, bank, receivable, payable, inventory, capital, revenue and COGS. System posting looks up accounts by display name. | Seed legacy chart codes and control identifiers. Preserve account master and reports. Refer to permanent control IDs. |
| Customer/supplier master | Lists/adds parties with type, name, phone and address. Parties are not separate ledger accounts. Invoice screen can add a matching party inline. | Tenant-scoped party records and inline create. AR/AP control line carries party ID. |
| Opening balances | Combined editable party list with opening date. The latest source accepts both directions for account and party openings and uses Opening Balance Equity for the offset. Earlier notes describe Owner Capital as the offset for customer and supplier entries. Re-save replaces opening voucher; zero removes it. Opening inventory quantity/value is tracked separately from GL. | Post balanced opening entries and synchronized inventory opening stock. Preserve prior versions and reverse posted entries. Confirm opening date and offset rules before posting. |
| Inventory and stock ledger | Item master with unit, opening qty and rate. Live view includes opening, purchases, sales, on-hand, moving average and value. Stock ledger displays movements and running quantity. Arithmetic uses REAL/float and dated stock valuation differs from available-stock test. | Normalize units and valuation; consistent dated availability and costing using exact decimals. Preserve movement and running balances. |
| Manual voucher | JV, VR, CPV, BPV, CRV and BRV; two equal lines. Party selected within debit/credit dropdown, most recent notes supersede a standalone party selector. Receipts require customer credit; payments require supplier debit. Cash/bank suggested where applicable. Shows current party balance. | Preserve types and party side rules, support balanced journals with validated company, dates, accounts, party and permissions. Use atomic posting and safe sequences. |
| Repair legacy party link | Lists unlinked control-account lines, allows explicit attachment to same-company customer/supplier. Cannot infer original party safely. | Authorized repair with evidence, before/after audit and review of affected subledger totals. |
| Sales/purchase invoices | Multi-line entry and update/removal of unsaved rows; inline party/item creation; totals and stock validation. Purchase Dr inventory / Cr payable with weighted receipt cost. Sales Dr receivable / Cr sales and Dr COGS / Cr inventory with dated moving average. Invoice rows repeat header fields. Numbering uses counts and dates. | Normalized headers/lines and linked posting; preserve line editing, stock checks, control entries, prices, quantity and totals. Serialized allocation of document numbers. |
| Voucher and invoice registers | Lists posted records, with invoices shown by line. Invoice delete permanently removes document, voucher and stock records and can invalidate later valuation. | Preserve registers, add traceable revision/reversal and authorized restoration subject to period and valuation rules. |
| General and party ledgers | Select account or search/select party. Date filters, opening, dated detail, running and closing balance. Party ledger filters tagged lines on the correct control account. General Ledger calculates opening as pre-range debits alone, overstating balances where opening credits exist. | Same reports from authorized posted journals; deterministic order and decimal balance calculations. |
| Debtors/creditors aging | Per-company report allocates eligible control-side totals FIFO by invoice date and number. It can count credits/debits unrelated to settlement and uses invoice date for age. Combined report is separately based on positive net AR/AP per party as of date, grouped by company with totals. | Preserve both report meanings. Use persisted payment allocations for aging, with review of fallback FIFO and invoice age policy. Preserve as-of totals and distinct combined report. |
| Trial balance, P&L and balance sheet | Aggregates ledger lines; Trial balance shows gross debit/credit by main head. P&L distinguishes sales, COGS, gross profit, other income, operating expenses and net. Purchase-account activity is excluded from the P&L net but included in balance-sheet earnings. Balance sheet also reports its reconciliation difference. | Reconcile to posted journals, period/date context and control totals; preserve statement sections. |
| Daily MIS | Defaults month to date; filters by account type or head, shows accounts with activity, opening and period debit/credit and closing net split Dr/Cr. | Preserve filter, scope and report columns. |
| Combined company report | Final Python source groups active company sections in one report ordered Azam, Sakoon/Khawab, others. Earlier notes also describe a consolidated matching-head view; final source no longer displays that version. | Preserve company-wise and consolidated views. Apply per-company report authorization and confirm account mappings before consolidation. |
| Report preview and printing | Tkinter tables and browser previews from temporary HTML. Printed reports are supported; README explicitly excludes PDF/print invoices. | Authorized browser printing and server-generated reports/PDF/Excel in reporting phase. |
| Backup, restore, database check | User-selected local .db copies with validation and prior safety copy; restore replaces all companies. Batch script tests the desktop app’s DB. | Neon recovery window plus independent scheduled encrypted backups, isolated restore drills, and logged restore operations. |
| Data upgrades and dates | Startup adds missing SQLite line columns, normalizes legacy dates and seeds renamed companies. Document entry accepts DD-MM-YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY/MM/DD. | Forward migrations in PostgreSQL, explicit import with verified IDs and reconciliations; display DD-MM-YYYY and keep date-only accounting fields. |

## Manual register photographs

- **Day Book** (filename without suffix) shows Sr #, Date, Vr Type, Description/Supplier/Customer, Dr, Cr and Bal. Handwriting repeats dates and seems to identify company shorthand in the voucher-type column. The photograph cannot establish whether the balance belongs to cash, a selected account or a combined view. Preserve the column intent and confirm reporting semantics.
- **Sale / Purchase** (filename ending in `(1).jpeg`) shows Sr #, Date, Vr/Inv #, Description/Supplier/Customer, VR Type, Purch Amount and Sale Amount, with a totals row. The sheet is blank. Confirm returns presentation and whether manual voucher rows should enter the register automatically.
- Desktop setup, executable packaging and manual database copying are historical deployment details. Bank reconciliation, automated tax, audit trails and migration tooling were explicitly absent from the V3 README.

## Original company aliases

The desktop startup maps `AZAM ENTERPRISE` to `AZAM ENTERPRISES`, `AZAM TRADERS` to `SAKOON / KHAWAB`, and `AZAM SERVICES` to `ABDULLAH TRADERS` if the respective new name is absent. Historical imports should resolve those aliases with a reviewed legacy-ID mapping.
